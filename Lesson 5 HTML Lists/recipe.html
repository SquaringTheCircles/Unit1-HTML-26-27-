Spanish Paella

I picked Spanish Paella because it is generally a combination of the two cultures that I come from. The first being from my father's side, being from Spain, and the second being from my mother's side, being from the Philippines. For my father's side, whenever we go to Spain, being once a year for 1 month almost immediately after school ends, we basically are almost guarenteed to eat paella from my grandparents.

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup Extra virgin olive Oil
  • 1 diced onion
  • 1 diced bell pepper
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 3 very finely diced roma tomatoes
  • Bay leaf
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 pinch saffron threads
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1/4 cup white wine
  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut to pieces
  • 1/4 cup fresh chopped parsley, divided
  • 2 cups Spanish Rice
  • 5 cups Chicken Broth
  • 1/2 lb Jumbo Shrimp or prawns (peeled with tail on)
  • 1/2 lb Mussels, cleaned properly
  • 8 oz calamari rings
  • Lemons, for garnish

Steps

  1. Add olive oil to a skillet over medium heat. Add the onion, bell peppers, and garlic, and cook all of them together until the onion is translucent.
  2. Add chopped tomato, bay leaf, paprika, saffron salt, and pepper. Stir all for 5 minutes.
  3. Add white wine and cook for 10 minutes.
  4. Add salt if needed.
  5. Add chicken pieces, 2 tablespoons of the chopped parsley, as well as more rice to the pot. Cook all contents for a minute.
  6. Pour broth slowly all throughout the pan and shake it to get the rice even. (Don't stir beyond this point.)
  7. Bring all to boil.
  8. Reduce the heat to minimum low. Give the pan a shake back and forth once or twice between the previous step and this step.
  9. Cook for about 15-18 minutes, then add the shrimp, calamari, and musseels, and then add peas after that.
  10. Cook for about 5 more minutes while the liquid becomes absorbed.
  11. If rice still isn't cooked, add 1/4 cup of either water or broth and continue cooking the mix.
  12. Remove the skillet from heat.
  13. Cover the pan with lid/tinfoil, place a towel over it and let it rest for 10 minutes.
  14. Garnish the paella with parsley and lemon slices.
  15. Serve to extended family.

Notes

  • Ingredient Notes
    • Spanish rice is traditional within Paella, and that's what works best with this recipe. If it is necessary, you may end up substituting it with medium grain rice (ex. Calrose rice). If you do, reduce the broth to 3.75 cups.
    • You can use many different types of meat within this recipe, including pork, turkey, rabbit, chrizo, or multiple at once.
    • For convenience, I would recommend that you use chicken broth, however if you somehow have the time, you can make your own fish stock from discarded shells of seafood to make a very authentic paella.
    • You can leave out the seafood and substitute chicken or vegetables if you dislike it, and you can also use any combo of your favorite seafood. Alternatively, you can use frozen seafood, but make sure to thaw it first, as well as making sure it doesnt have an extremely strong fishy odor, as well as clean it properly.
    • Make sure to buy high quality Saffron for the best results, however if necessary substitute it with a teaspoon of saffron powder.
  • Adaptation Variations
    • As discussed above, if needed, you can substitue it with medium grain rice, however you must reduce the chicken stock in the recipe to a total of 3.75 cups.
    • If you substitute boneless pork loin, you must cut it into 1/2 inch cubes, brown the pieces in the hot oil, and then set it aside. You then have to add it into the pan within step 3 before boiling
    • If you substitute turkey or rabbit, you similarly have to brown the meat in the hot oil, set it aside, and add it back into the pan in step 3 before boiling.
    • If you substitute chorizo, you add 1 chorizo sausage cut into 1/2 inch disks, brown it into hot oil as discussed prior, set it aside, add it back in step 3 before boiling.
    • Vegetarian Paella consists omitted meat and seafood, as well as adding more vegetables including artichoke, green beans, mushroom, olives, and asparagus, and adding all of them in step 1 with bell peppers.
    • Valencian Paella is made with rabbit, artichokes, chicken, and green beans.
